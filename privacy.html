Privacy Policy

Angels Touch · Effective 18 November 2026

In short: Angels Touch plays the music that is already on your device. It does not collect, upload, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Who we are

Angels Touch is an offline music player app developed by Nikhil ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app, on the web or as an Android app.

Your music and files

When you choose songs or a folder, the app reads those files directly on your device to play them and to read their details (title, artist, album, cover art and lyrics). Your music files are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To remember your preferences, the app saves a small amount of information in your device's own browser storage: favorites, volume, theme, equalizer and sleep-timer settings, lyrics you add or that were found for a song, and (on computers that support it) access to the music folder you chose. This information stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Online lyrics search

If a song has no lyrics on your device, and "Auto-find online" is on, or you tap "Search online", the app sends the song's title, artist and length to LRCLIB, a free public lyrics library, to look up the lyrics. No other information about you is sent. LRCLIB, like any website, receives your device's IP address when it answers the request. You can turn this off with the "Auto-find online" switch in the Lyrics tab.

Fonts and app files

The app's files are delivered from GitHub Pages, and its fonts come from Google Fonts. As with any website, these services receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when the files are downloaded. After the first visit, the app keeps a copy of its files on your device so it can work offline.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not access your microphone, camera or contacts.

Children

Angels Touch does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
